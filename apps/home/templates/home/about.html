Our story

Timeless jewellery,
crafted for every day.

Maira Bijouterie was born in Casablanca with a simple belief: luxury jewelry shouldn't be out of reach. We bring together gold-plated, stainless steel and sterling silver pieces finished to a high standard — so you can wear them every day, without worry.

Every item in our collection is chosen for its finish, durability and everyday wearability. Waterproof and tarnish-resistant pieces are our signature — jewelry that keeps up with your life, not the other way around.

From our atelier to your door across Morocco, we offer cash on delivery, bank transfer and personal support over WhatsApp — in French and Arabic.
